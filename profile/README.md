### [Notifiable](https://notifiable.sh) is engagement infrastructure for developers and AI agents.

You build the app. Your agent — or your product — keeps users coming back. [Notifiable](https://notifiable.sh) is how you ask for permission, register devices, identify people, and send push that actually lands: scheduled, segmented, automated, with delivery and opens you can trust.

Connect once with a public key from [notifiable.sh](https://notifiable.sh). The same surface works across mobile stacks — request permission, identify the subscriber, read the inbox, track events, open deep links. Android and web delivery are handled for you; iOS needs your Apple Developer account linked once in the [dashboard](https://notifiable.sh).

Ship with the SDK that matches your stack:

- **Expo** — [`@notifiable/expo`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@notifiable/expo) · [source](https://github.com/itsnotifiable/expo)
- **React Native** — [`@notifiable/react-native`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@notifiable/react-native) · [source](https://github.com/itsnotifiable/react-native)
- **Flutter** — [`notifiable_flutter`](https://pub.dev/packages/notifiable_flutter) · [source](https://github.com/itsnotifiable/flutter)
- **Swift** — [itsnotifiable/swift](https://github.com/itsnotifiable/swift)
- **Kotlin** — [itsnotifiable/kotlin](https://github.com/itsnotifiable/kotlin)

Start a project, grab a development key, and wire it up at **[notifiable.sh](https://notifiable.sh)**.
